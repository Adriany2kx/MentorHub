# MentorHub — Design Context

> Single source of truth for all design decisions. All design skills (distill, harden, arrange, animate, clarify, delight, colorize, typeset, etc.) must load this file before making any UI changes.

---

## Design Context

### Users

**Primary**: Early-career professionals and people changing industries — 22–35 year olds who feel stuck, overwhelmed, or directionless about their career path. They're not beginners at life, but they're beginners at *this* transition. They arrive with anxiety and leave with a plan.

**Secondary**: Experienced professionals acting as mentors — credible, time-poor, looking for a clean tool that doesn't waste their time.

**Context of use**: Typically used at home, evenings or weekends, during a moment of deliberate self-investment. Not a quick-glance app — users arrive with intent.

**Job to be done**: Find a trusted expert who has walked the path the user wants to walk, book time with them, and leave feeling like forward motion is possible.

---

### Brand Personality

**Three words**: Warm · Trustworthy · Energising

- **Warm**: Human connection comes first. Every screen should feel like talking to a knowledgeable friend, not filling out a government form.
- **Trustworthy**: Credibility through restraint. Substance over flash. Mentor credentials, real reviews, clear pricing — no dark patterns, no fake urgency.
- **Energising**: The platform should leave users feeling *more* capable than when they arrived. Forward-looking language, progress signals, momentum.

**Voice and tone**: Direct and encouraging. Confident without being arrogant. Plain language — no buzzwords, no corporate speak, no inspirational-poster clichés.

---

### Emotional Target

> "When someone books their first mentor session, they should feel **confident and relieved** — like paying off a huge debt."

This is the north star. The platform must *dissolve anxiety*. Users arrive stressed about their careers; they should leave feeling like the path forward is clear and reachable. Every design decision should be filtered through: *does this reduce cognitive load and build trust, or does it add noise?*

**This means:**
- Clear information hierarchy — "what do I do next?" is always obvious
- No dark patterns, no fake scarcity, no aggressive CTAs
- Social proof (credentials, reviews, session counts) before conversion asks
- Empty states that teach, not just say "nothing here"
- Error messages that help, not punish

---

### Aesthetic Direction

**Style**: Warm Editorial Studio — the credibility of a respected professional publication, the human warmth of an independent bookshop.

**Reference feel**: LinkedIn's trustworthy structure × the3key.com's warmth and personality
- From LinkedIn: Clean hierarchy, professional credentialing, clear navigation, data density done well
- From the3key.com: Warm colour palette, editorial typography with personality, organic energy, not corporate cold

**Anti-references / what to avoid**:
- ❌ **AI slop**: Cyan-on-dark, purple-to-blue gradients, neon accents, glassmorphism everywhere — instantly signals "no real design thought went here"
- ❌ **Flashy = scammy**: Oversaturated palettes, aggressive urgency ("Only 2 spots left!"), excessive animation, rainbow gradients — destroys the trust the product depends on
- ❌ **Corporate cold**: Pure grey/blue LinkedIn clone, stock-photo professionalism, soulless enterprise aesthetic
- ❌ **Childish playful**: Rounded bubbly fonts (Fredoka, Nunito), claymorphism, emoji icons — wrong audience, undermines credibility

---

### Design System

**Typography**:
- Display/Editorial: **Fraunces** (variable serif — gorgeous italic for emotional emphasis, used on hero headlines, pull quotes, milestone celebrations)
- Body/UI: **DM Sans** (clean, friendly, legible — used for all functional UI text)
- Use Fraunces *sparingly* for maximum impact — italic for key words, not entire paragraphs

**Colour Palette**:

| Token | Light Mode | Dark Mode | Role |
|-------|-----------|-----------|------|
| `--color-bg` | `#F7F8FF` | `#0C0D1E` | Page background — barely-indigo white |
| `--color-surface` | `#FFFFFF` | `#151628` | Cards / panels |
| `--color-border` | `#E2E6F4` | `#2A2C4A` | Borders |
| `--color-ink` | `#1A1B2E` | `#E8EAFF` | Primary text |
| `--color-ink-2` | `#474D7A` | `#9BA3D0` | Secondary text |
| `--color-ink-3` | `#6B7499` | `#6068A0` | Muted/captions |
| `--color-blue` | `#4F46E5` | `#818CF8` | Primary / links / CTA (indigo) |
| `--color-hero-bg` | `#13143C` | `#07080F` | Dark hero/CTA/sidebar sections |

- Note: The `--color-blue` token carries the indigo primary for historical compatibility — all link/button/CTA elements reference it
- WCAG AA: indigo-600 (#4F46E5) is 6.2:1 on white; dark mode indigo-400 (#818CF8) is 5.0:1 on surface

**Border radius**: 6px (sm) · 12px (md) · 16px (lg)

**Shadows**: Indigo-tinted with `rgba(26,27,46,...)` in light mode; pure black alpha in dark mode

**Dark mode**: `[data-theme="dark"]` on `<html>` — persisted via `localStorage` and `prefers-color-scheme` fallback. Toggled via Navbar sun/moon button.

### Design Principles

1. **Clarity dissolves anxiety**
   Users arrive stressed. Information hierarchy is the primary design tool. Every page must answer "what do I do next?" without hunting. Prioritise scannability over cleverness.

2. **Warmth through restraint**
   The terracotta palette signals humanity without shouting. Use it as an accent, not wallpaper. Generous white space is part of the warmth — it signals that nothing is being hidden.

3. **Trust before conversion**
   Social proof (mentor credentials, verified reviews, session counts, ratings) must appear before conversion asks. Users need to believe before they commit. Never push a CTA before establishing credibility.

4. **Editorial confidence**
   Typography does the heavy lifting. Fraunces italic for gravitas moments (hero headlines, testimonials, milestone celebrations). DM Sans for everything functional. No decorative clutter — every visual element must earn its place.

5. **Energising, not exhausting**
   Animations are purposeful and swift (150–700ms max). Motion signals progress and responds to actions — it never performs for its own sake. The platform should feel alive but never chaotic. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

---

### Accessibility

- **Target**: WCAG AA minimum across all pages
- **Colour contrast**: All text tokens verified at ≥4.5:1 on their respective backgrounds
- **Motion**: All animations wrapped with `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` overrides (already in `index.css`)
- **Touch targets**: Minimum 44×44px for all interactive elements (validated in Navbar dark mode toggle)
- **Focus states**: Visible `outline: 2px solid var(--color-blue)` with `outline-offset: 2px` on all interactive elements
- **Semantic HTML**: Form labels, alt text, aria-labels on icon-only buttons
- **Additional requirements (confirmed April 2026)**: No additional accessibility constraints beyond the standards above
